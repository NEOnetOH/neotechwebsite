Venue & Parking

Kent State University Student Center

1075 Risman Dr, Kent, OH 44242

PARKING INFO

Primary Parking (Validated)

Please park in the Student Center Visitor Lot directly in front of the Kiva Auditorium off of Risman Dr. Parking validation will be provided.

Overflow Parking (Free)

If the Visitor Lot is full, you may park in Lot R-12 located on the other side of Summit St. This lot is free to park in.

CHECK-IN DIRECTIONS
  1. 1

    After parking, walk towards the large plaza area, following signs for the Student Center.

  2. 2

    Enter the Student Center and walk past the cafeteria.

  3. 3

    Go up the stairs or elevator to the 2nd floor to check in for the conference.
